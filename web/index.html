Applications

On: the application starts at its start time. A producer that is off is not deployed, so nothing announces its data; switching it on restarts the run with it. Switching a running producer off or on disconnects or connects it at once. m2 can be a relay, a producer or a consumer.

Nodes and links

On: the node's radio (off keeps its tables). CS: caching. Clear empties the Content Store or the FIB (the PIT cannot be emptied in NFD). Links: in range or not. Click a node or a link on the map for loss, cost, routes and every table.

Transmission efficiency

Minimum Interest frames: for every Data a consumer received, one Interest per hop on the shortest path to the node that served it (producer or cache), with the links in range at that moment. Efficiency is that minimum divided by the Interest frames actually sent, so 100% means no Interest was wasted. Suppressed: retransmissions a node did not forward because they came within the suppression interval. Lost: frames a node sent that no one received.
